Home is a saved commanded position, not sensor-based homing. Initial limits are 1400–1600 µs; verify your mechanics before enabling.

Wiring & joint identification

G → black/brown ground · S → white/orange/yellow signal. Red → external regulated servo positive at the servo's rated voltage. Join supply negative to Nano GND. Do not route eight arm servos' power through USB or the Nano regulator.
