        About the twin

        The body is Gael Langevin's original VinMoov assembly (the virtual InMoov used by MyRobotLab): every part sits where the designer placed it, at 1 : 1 scale, with the real joint pivots. Colours follow the source file — white printed shells, black servos and hardware, bronze bearings — with orange fingers to match your build.

        Robot mode animates the twin from acknowledged pulses through your twin calibration. It is an expected pose, not encoder feedback or collision checking.
