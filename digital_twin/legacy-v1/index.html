    MODEL & FEEDBACK

    Built from InMoov.

    Original design by Gael Langevin. Articulated geometry from the InMoov community.

    Official i2 head, eye and hand meshes and revised three-piston neck parts are fitted to the articulated reference body. Fingers are orange; servos and joint hardware are black. These assembly placements are visual fits, not verified CAD mates or construction measurements. Use the original STL dimensions and official assembly guides when building.

    Eight arm joints map to the Nano. Head, fingers, torso and legs are visual references until additional hardware is mapped.

    Robot mode animates acknowledged commands through your visual angle mapping. It is an estimated pose, not encoder feedback, collision detection or a physics simulation.
